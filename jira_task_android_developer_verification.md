# Jira Task: Complete Android developer verification registration

**Issue type:** Task  
**Priority:** High  
**Due date:** 2026-09-30  
**Assignee:** Unassigned  

## Summary

Register all Android apps and signing keys required for Google Play’s Android developer verification deadline.

## Description

Google Play requires apps to be registered by **September 30, 2026**. Apps that are not registered may be removed from Google Play globally. From that date, apps distributed through participating third-party stores in select regions must also be registered by a verified developer to remain installable on certified Android devices.

Complete the following in Play Console:

1. Review the Play Console Home page for unregistered package names.
2. Finish any draft package-name registrations already started.
3. Register any apps distributed outside Google Play.
4. Add any additional signing keys used to sign Play apps outside Google Play.
5. Confirm the developer account verification and registration statuses are complete.

No separate Android Developer Console account is required.

## Acceptance criteria

- All Play apps intended for continued distribution show a successful registration status.
- Any draft package-name registrations are completed successfully.
- All apps distributed outside Google Play are registered.
- All additional signing keys used for external distribution are registered.
- Developer verification is complete, with no unresolved warnings or blocked registrations in Play Console.
- Evidence of completion is attached to this Jira task, such as screenshots or an export of the final registration status.
